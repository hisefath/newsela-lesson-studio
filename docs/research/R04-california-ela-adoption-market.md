# R04 California grades 6–8 ELA/ELD market: adoption timeline, competitors, customer, size

## Answer

California is running a 2026 ELA/ELD *follow-up* adoption for TK–8 with final State Board action scheduled for November 2026; the last ELA/ELD adoption was November 2015, and newly adopted programs are added to the 2015 list rather than replacing it. For grades 6–8, CDE review panels recommended (advisory, not yet approved) programs from McGraw Hill, HMH, Savvas, Imagine Learning (StudySync), ThinkCERCA, Inquiry by Design, Open Up Resources (grades 7–8 only) and Newsela. With about 1.31 million California public students in grades 6–8 (Fall 2022) and an assumed $25–$60 per student per year for a core ELA program, statewide grade 6–8 core ELA spend is roughly $33M–$79M a year (base about $53M); the price input and which districts are the best first customers are not settled by a Tier-A source. Confidence: medium, because the timeline, competitors and enrollment are Tier A but the price rests on one out-of-state Tier-B data point. (S04.1, S01.4, S04.3–S04.12, S03.2, S03.3, S04.13, S04.14)

## Takeaway

California picks approved English textbooks about once a decade, the next vote is November 2026, about eight publishers are competing for middle school, and that market is worth roughly fifty million dollars a year.

## Findings

- **F1** [LB] In July 2025 the SBE initiated a follow-up adoption of ELA/ELD instructional materials for TK–8; "The final adoption ... is scheduled to take place in November 2026" — S04.1 (A), Summary of the Issue(s)
- **F2** [LB] The SBE last acted on ELA/ELD instructional materials on November 4, 2015; the current adopted list "includes only those materials that were developed for the 2015 adoption" — S04.1 (A), Brief History of Key Issues
- **F3** [LB] Programs adopted in November 2026 "will be added to the 2015 list"; both existing and new materials stay eligible for purchase until a new or revised framework adoption (EC 60200[h]) — S01.4 (A), Introduction
- **F4** [LB] Publishers may not change submitted materials after May 15, 2026 (delivery date) except through the edits-and-corrections process; after adoption, revisions happen only in a fee-based process "approximately every two years" (5 CCR 9526). Fee: $8,000 per grade level for Program Types 1–3; prices go on CDE's online Price List and cannot rise for two years — S01.4 (A), Program Types and Participation Fees; Price List; Price Increases
- **F5** [LB] CDE review-panel advisory reports for grades 6–8, each marked "has not been approved by the Instructional Quality Commission or the State Board of Education", recommend: McGraw Hill *Summit! California* (Type 2); HMH *Into Literature California* (Type 2); Savvas *myPerspectives ELA with myVoice ELD* (Type 2) and *myPerspectives ELA California* (Type 1); Imagine Learning *StudySync California* (Types 1 and 2); ThinkCERCA Core ELAR (Types 1 and 2); Inquiry by Design ELA with ELD California Edition (Type 2); Open Up Resources *EL Education* (Type 1, grades 7–8 only); Newsela *Threads & Themes* (Types 1 and 2) — S04.3 to S04.12 (A), S03.2 (A), S03.3 (A), report headers and Recommendation
- **F6** [LB] California public enrollment, Fall 2022: grade 6 438,570; grade 7 440,251; grade 8 436,134 (sum 1,314,955) — S04.13 (A), California row
- **F7** Shawnee Mission (Kansas) approved $3.5M for McGraw-Hill K–6 ELA (StudySync for grade 6) for about 14,400 students over six years, "roughly $40" per student a year, with print, digital and PD included — S04.14 (B), single source, so not load-bearing alone
- **F8** The 2026 adoption covers six program types and allows "materials that address subsets of standards" (e.g. foundational reading, media literacy) — S04.1 (A), Brief History of Key Issues; S01.4 (A), fee schedule

## Inferences

- **I1** from F6 + F7: annual core ELA spend, CA grades 6–8. Formula: 1,314,955 students × $40 per student per year = $52.6M, about $53M per year. Assumptions: A-1 every public grade 6–8 student is covered by one paid core ELA program; A-2 price $40/student/year (F7, a 2022 K–6 Kansas deal, USD nominal); A-3 Fall 2022 enrollment holds roughly flat. Range: low $33M (price $25) – high $79M (price $60), base $53M per year.
- **I2** from I1 + F7: value of one adoption cycle. Formula: $53M per year × 6-year license = $320M per cycle. Assumptions: A-1 to A-3 above; A-4 districts buy six-year licenses as in F7. Range: low $200M – high $470M, base $320M.
- **I3** from I1: obtainable share (SOM) for one new entrant. Formula: 1,314,955 × share s × $40. Assumptions: A-1 to A-3; A-5 s = 5% of students in the first cycle (open assumption, no adoption benchmark found). Range: low $1.1M (s 2%) – high $5.3M (s 10%), base $2.6M per year.
- **I4** from F2 + F3: most California districts using state-adopted grade 6–8 ELA materials are on programs built for the 2015 adoption, about ten years old, and the November 2026 list is the first chance in a decade to buy newly adopted ones. High for the timeline; which districts are actually shopping is unknown.
- **I5** from F5: Newsela competes against at least seven other recommended grade 6–8 publishers, most offering a Type 2 (ELA plus ELD) program as Newsela does. High.
- **I6** from F4: a publisher's adopted content is frozen from May 2026 and revised only in a roughly two-yearly fee-based window, so lesson production and approval for California happens in bursts tied to those windows. Medium.
